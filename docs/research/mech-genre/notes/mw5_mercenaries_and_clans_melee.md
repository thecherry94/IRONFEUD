# MechWarrior 5: Mercenaries (2019-2026) and MechWarrior 5: Clans (2024-2026): melee, physicality, structure, reception

Scope: what is specific to the two newest Piranha Games titles, with melee emphasised. Franchise names appear only as citations.
Research date: 2026-11-02. Source quality: patch notes, store/press pages, PC Gamer and Sarna reviews and the Sarna wiki are primary or near-primary.
Steam and Reddit threads are anecdotal and labelled as such. Fandom and Nexus pages refused fetches (HTTP 402/403, or rendered empty).

---

## 1. Melee: how it works in MW5 Mercenaries and MW5 Clans, how it was received, and what came before it

### Takeaway
MW5 Mercenaries shipped in 2019 with no melee. Critics singled this out: mechs that collide "awkwardly grind against each other". Melee arrived on 23 Sept 2021 as a free update. It is deliberately simple: one button (weapon group 5), a fixed arm swing, damage that scales with weight class, and a ~4-5 s swing cycle. A built-in "punch" exists only on mechs that have hand or lower-arm actuators. The Call to Arms DLC (June 2022) later added equippable physical weapons in dedicated melee slots.
It has no knockdown, stagger, kick or charge mechanic, and that is the main thing players say is missing. Opinion is split between "meme / waste of tonnage" and "sleeper OP on fast jump-jet or melee-quirked mechs". The AI uses melee only opportunistically.
MW5 Clans keeps the same model and adds a per-pilot melee skill. I found no evidence that Clans added new melee mechanics.

### Cited Findings
**When it arrived and what the base system is (Mercenaries)**
- PC Gamer's 2019 launch review (Jonathan Bolding, 80/100) said melee is "sorely missing". When mechs collide they "awkwardly grind against each other". The starting mech's prominent claw-and-shield arm "just hangs limply". He calls it notable because "every FPS for 15 years has included a token melee attack". At launch you could still run over smaller tanks and do Death From Above by landing on others with jump jets, "both have suitably satisfying results" — [PC Gamer MW5 Mercenaries review](https://www.pcgamer.com/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-review/)
- The free "Melee" update launched on 23 Sept 2021 alongside the Legend of the Kestrel Lancers DLC and the PlayStation release. Its patch notes say melee punch weapons are "permanently fixed to 'Mechs that have hand or lower arm actuators", carry no repair costs, and are "mapped to weapon group five" (adjusted for gamepad face buttons). You can "direct your punches to different locations on enemy 'Mechs". The notes pitch it as "Great for close-quarters combat, or when attacking would otherwise overheat you". New arm-swing and impact sound effects were added — [Gematsu, 2021-09](https://gematsu.com/2021/09/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-expansion-legend-of-the-kestrel-lancers-and-free-melee-update-launch-september-23)
- Sarna's wiki describes the melee update as "a first for a MechWarrior video game". Call to Arms added nine melee weapons (hatchets, swords, maces, claws) and 23 melee variants — [Sarna wiki: MechWarrior 5: Mercenaries](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Mercenaries)
- Call to Arms (8 June 2022) added 9 weapons (BattleAxe, Claymore, Katana, Axe, Trench Blade, Long Sword, Hatchet, Claw, Mace), 23 standard and 5 hero melee variants, and a new hatchet-mech chassis with a 5-mission quest line — [Steam: Call to Arms](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1969380/MechWarrior_5_Mercenaries__Call_to_Arms/); [GOGDB release notes 2022-06-07](https://www.gogdb.org/product/2147483045/releasenotes)
- Developer intent at Call to Arms: melee is "the headlining feature". The team says melee was first introduced with Kestrel Lancers and that they "monitored its rollout" to find issues. The goal was to make melee "a more essential part of the skill set" for close-range brawling. — [mw5mercs.com Call to Arms launch intel, 2022-06](https://mw5mercs.com/news/2022/06/73-call-to-arms-launch-date-intel)
- **A notable balancing lever: they slowed the short-range missile cooldown so a melee strike fits between volleys.** SRM2 went from 2.4 to 2.8 s, and SRM4/SRM6 (and the Artemis versions) from 2.4 to 3.4 s. The stated intent is to "create room for a full melee strike between SRM cooldown cycles". The change also applies to hostile units, and "Stream SRMs" were deliberately left unchanged — [mw5mercs.com Call to Arms intel](https://mw5mercs.com/news/2022/06/73-call-to-arms-launch-date-intel)

**Numbers: damage by weight class, swing rate and reach** (Sarna wiki equipment table, taken from game files. It notes rate of fire "is indicated in the game's files but is not currently displayed anywhere in-game") — [Sarna: MW5 Mercenaries/Equipment](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Mercenaries/Equipment)
- Built-in strikes. Damage scales by weight class, and having a hand doubles it:
  - Unequipped / lower arm: Light 5, Medium 7.5, Heavy 10, Assault 12.5.
  - Hand or barrel-fist actuator: 10 / 15 / 20 / 25.
  - Battle-fist actuator: Medium 20, Heavy 25, Assault 30.
  - Swing rate is about 14.1 swings/min for lights and 12.5 for assaults, i.e. a cycle of about 4.3-4.8 s.
  - Reach is 17.5 m for every class.
- Equippable fist items (one per arm slot). All values are 0.5 t lights / 1 t mediums / 1.5 t heavies / 2 t assaults:
  - Knuckles: Light 10-27.5, Medium 15-32.5, Heavy 20-37.5, Assault 25-42.5 damage (tiers 0-5).
  - Hammerfist (for handless arms): 5 damage lower in every case.
  - Arena Fist: much heavier (2/4/5/7 t) and much faster. Lights swing 30/min, mediums 24/min, heavies 20/min, assaults 17.1/min.
- Physical weapons, tier 0 to tier 5:

  | Weapon (class) | Tonnage | Damage | Swings/min | Reach |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Hatchet (medium) | 3 t | 30 → 52.5 | ~12.6-15.5 | 20 m |
  | Mace (medium) | 4 t | 40 → 62.5 | 12-14.5 | 20 m |
  | Claw (medium) | 4 t | 20 → 42.5 | 17-23 | 18.75 m |
  | Lance (medium) | 5 t | 28 → 45.5 | — | — |
  | Axe (heavy) | 5 t | 50 → 72.5 | 11.2-13.3 | 20 m |
  | Longsword (heavy) | 5 t | 40 → 62.5 | — | — |
  | Trench Blade (heavy) | 4 t | 30 → 52.5 | up to 24 | 18.75 m |
  | Hammer (heavy) | 7 t | 56 → 94.5 | 9.7-13.5 | 20 m |
  | Katana (assault) | 6 t | 45 → 67.5 | — | 22.5 m |
  | Claymore (assault) | 6 t | 60 → 75+ (table truncated past tier 3) | — | 20 m |

  So there are two archetypes: slow and heavy-hitting (mace, axe, hammer) versus fast and lighter-hitting (claw, trench blade, arena fist).
- Players report the same figures independently: "22.5 damage per hand for .5 tons" for light knuckles, and "assault knuckles are 2 tons a piece" — [Reddit r/Mechwarrior5 "are melee weapons OP" (via search snippet)](https://pol1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/Mechwarrior5/comments/1j3b9y1/are_melee_weapons_op)

**Slots and access (Mercenaries)**
- Physical weapons need "Large Melee" slots, which exist only on the DLC melee variants. Knuckles go in a Medium hand slot, and Hammerfists in the Small slot of handless mechs. The base game does not allow dual-wielding — [Steam: Cannot equip melee weapons](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/6629936499888377392/); [Steam: Knuckles](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/5796736618327550884/); [Steam: melee discussion 2024-10](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/4692280570914398205)
- Modders say PGI "chose to only add melee hardpoints to mechs with hands". The popular YAML mod added "dual-wield melee support", a large expansion of hardpoints, and melee quirks. Companion mods give handless mechs melee weapons, but the weapons stay fixed because of "the crude way hardpoints are implemented" — [Search summary of YAML Steam changelogs](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2549720490); [Nexus mod 955 (via search)](https://www.nexusmods.com/mechwarrior5mercenaries/mods/955/)

**Self-damage, collisions and technique (anecdotal, Steam, Feb 2023)**
- Players report self-damage from "bumping/scraping/ramming". Arm damage can occur "when swinging melee at heavier mechs". Melee range is given as 17.5 m (weapons 17.5-22 m) with a ~4.5 m "safe zone". The community technique is "jousting": run past the target on your melee side, twist the torso and strike as you pass, stopping short so you don't collide. Some reports may be co-op desync — [Steam: Taking damage from own melee attacks?](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/3768985982150039057/)
- "Ramming or meleeing causes damage to both mechs, depending on speed and relative tonnage" (player claim) — [Steam: Mech knockdown thread (via search summary)](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/3975051032839426182/?l=english)

**Reception of melee (Mercenaries)**
- Opinion is split. Against: "waste of tonnage, time and C-bills"; "biggest disappointment"; "more of a meme than anything else". For: a melee mech "with the speed to use them" can outdamage LRM boats, and a heavy melee mech is "absolutely mental". Some weapons reportedly one-shot a full-health heavy or assault that has already lost a leg. Jump-jet hopping plus speed boosts is described as a distinct, fun playstyle. The AI "uses melee opportunistically rather than well". The melee sound design is praised (all player posts, Oct 2024) — [Steam: melee discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/4692280570914398205); [Steam: Not seeing the point of Call to Arms](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/3417685728347414866/)
- The risk trade-off players cite: "The high damage is balanced by needing to get to close & take more damage"; "If the enemy is packing A/C20's, I'm not going into melee" — [Reddit (via search)](https://pol1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/Mechwarrior5/comments/1j3b9y1/are_melee_weapons_op)
- Call to Arms is rated "Mixed" on Steam (~145 reviews at the time of the search) — [Steam store page (via search)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1969380)

**MW5 Clans melee**
- In Clans every pilot has melee as one of two shared base skills, which players find odd given the Clans' in-lore disdain for physical combat. One pilot deliberately has no melee skill for lights. The AI "will use melee attacks unless there are no fists available". Melee is "more practical for a light" because it is "free damage" and lights lack leg armour. "Melee is used to kick the tanks when you walk on them" (player claims, Oct 2024 - Feb 2025) — [Steam: MW5 Clans discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2000890/discussions/0/4691154661545253974)
- The pilot skill trees include "melee damage" next to handling, weapon cooldown and evasion — [GamingBolt: MW5 Clans everything you need to know](https://gamingbolt.com/mechwarrior-5-clans-everything-you-need-to-know)
- The Wolves of Tukayyid DLC (Dec 2025) gives one lancemate a "close combat" specialty. The same release made jump jets vectorable ("rocket your 'Mech in whatever direction you prefer"), and Sarna says this makes Death From Above "more viable". The jump-jet change is in the free base-game update — [Sarna: Wolves of Tukayyid review](https://www.sarna.net/news/wolves-of-tukayyid-review-raised-by-wolves/)
- Mercenaries' DLC 8, Chaos Reign (26 May 2026), also brought "jump jet thrust vectoring" to Mercenaries — [Sarna wiki: MW5 Mercenaries](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Mercenaries)

**Precedents: earlier games and tabletop**
- Tabletop BattleTech physical attacks (rulebook text), where "W" is the attacker's tonnage — [Rulespal: BattleTech A Game of Armored Combat rulebook](https://rulespal.com/battletech-a-game-of-armored-combat/rulebook):

  | Attack | Damage | Other rules |
  |---|---|---|
  | Punch | 1 per 10 t (W/10) | Halved for each damaged or missing arm actuator. Missing lower arm +2 to hit, missing hand +1. Hits use a punch location table (upper body). |
  | Kick | W/5 as one damage group | -2 to hit. **The target must make a piloting roll. A missed kick forces the kicker to roll.** Hits use a kick location table (legs). |
  | Club | W/5 | -1 to hit. Needs hands. |
  | Push | None | Target rolls or falls. |
  | Charge | Target takes (W/10) × hexes moved. Attacker takes 1 per 10 t of the *target* | Both roll at +2 or fall. |
  | Death From Above | Target takes (W/10) × 3. Attacker takes W/5 to the legs | Attacker rolls at +4. **A missed DFA is an automatic fall as if from 2 levels.** |

  - Piloting roll triggers: taking 20+ damage in one phase (+1), a destroyed leg or foot actuator (+1), a destroyed hip (+2), a gyro hit (+3), being kicked or pushed (0). A destroyed leg or gyro means an automatic fall.
  - Falling damage: 1 per 10 t × (levels + 1), with a random facing on landing.
- The tabletop hatchet does 1 damage per 5 t at -1 to hit — [Sarna: Hatchet (via search)](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/Hatchet)
- The hatchet mech (3023) was "the first 'Mech purpose-built for melee" — [Sarna: Physical attack](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/Physical_attack)
- MWO, PGI's earlier game, had no melee and only very small collision damage. Knockdown on collision existed in early beta and was removed. Players attribute the removal to abuse: one recalls fast mechs "knocking down assault mechs by colliding at over 100 kph" (forum claims, not PGI statements) — [Steam: Mech knockdown thread 2023-11](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/3975051032839426182/?l=english); [MWO forums 2014 (via search)](https://mwomercs.com/forums/topic/168441-any-time-line-on-collision-and-knockdowns/page__view__findpost__p__3652429)
- Players recall knockdown in MW3/MW4 and in MechAssault. In MechAssault it was "mainly tied to heavy ballistic weapons" such as gauss — [Steam: Mech knockdown thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/3975051032839426182/?l=english); [Steam MW5 Clans knockdown thread 2024-05](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/4355620416677363103)
- MW4 accounted for Death From Above in its jump rules, but landing one was "by guess and by God" without an external camera — [Quarter to Three: MechWarrior 4 retrospective (via search snippet)](https://quartertothree.com/features/robots/robots_6.shtml)
- In HBS's BattleTech (2018, tactics), DFA damages the attacker's legs and can make it fall whether or not it hits. Melee removes a target's evasion and does double damage to some vehicles. This is the melee showcase PC Gamer contrasted MW5 against — [MagicGameWorld BattleTech guide](https://www.magicgameworld.com/battletech-notes-about-physical-rather-than-ranged-attacks/); [PC Gamer MW5 review](https://www.pcgamer.com/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-review/)

### Inferences
- MW5's melee is essentially a **short-range, heat-free, ammo-free "weapon" with a ~4-5 s cooldown and ~17.5-22 m reach**. It adds no new rules: no stability, no fall, no piloting check. That is why it reads as "bolted on". It changes how much damage you deal, but not what can happen in a fight.
- MW5's built-in damage roughly follows the tabletop proportions: weight-class steps, with a hand doubling the punch. The tabletop's *interesting* parts (kick → the target must roll or fall; a missed kick or DFA → you fall; damage → piloting checks) were all dropped. Players' most repeated wish (knockdown/stagger) is exactly that dropped layer.
- Per-weapon DPS is comparable to a mid-weight gun with no heat. A tier-5 heavy axe is 72.5 dmg × 13.3/60 ≈ 16 DPS; a tier-5 assault knuckle is 42.5 × 14.4/60 ≈ 10 DPS. The cost is exposure and closing distance. The SRM cooldown change shows PGI thinking of melee as **a beat inside a brawler's firing rhythm** — swing between volleys — not as a separate mode.
- For IRONFEUD: melee is the place where a procedural-locomotion game can do what MW5 cannot. A strike can be a real momentum transfer into a spring-driven body (stagger as a footfall-like impulse, a balance check from support polygon or step recovery), rather than a canned swing with a damage number. A tabletop-style "the kick risks *your* balance too" rule gives lights a high-risk, high-reward leg-kick job against slow assaults. That matches CLAUDE.md goal 4 (lights keep jobs assaults can't do). The "jousting pass" technique players invented shows reach plus approach angle is already a skill dimension worth designing for deliberately.
- Making the AI melee only "opportunistically" reads as weak in MW5. Since AI pilots must use the same `MechInput`, melee needs an explicit utility consideration (closing cost vs. expected damage vs. counter-melee risk), plus visible wind-up intent to stay readable.

### Gaps
- No exact MW5 swing animation duration, wind-up, hit-detection method (trace along the arm vs. a cone), or rule for how "directing punches to locations" works. Is it the reticle point at swing time? Not found.
- I found no official statement on whether melee damages the attacker's own arm. Player reports conflict, and co-op desync is a suspected cause.
- Clans melee specifics (damage values, which OmniMechs have hands, skill percentages) were not found in reliable sources.
- I could not confirm MW3/MW4 knockdown or fall mechanics from primary sources. Only player recollection was found.

## 2. Falls, knockdowns, collisions, stomping and destruction in MW5 / MW5 Clans

### Takeaway
Neither game has player-facing knockdown or falls. Collisions do mutual damage scaled by speed and tonnage but otherwise just "grind". Small vehicles can be trampled. Death From Above exists implicitly through landing damage. Buildings and trees are destructible in both games, and Clans' Unreal Engine 5 physics (Chaos) makes size readable: assaults plough through buildings that lights use as cover.
"Legged" mechs in Mercenaries limp only briefly; PGI chose this as a co-op compromise. Knockdown is the most requested missing mechanic, and some modders have hacked it in.

### Cited Findings
- Clans has "the best physics of a MechWarrior game to date". Even a 25-ton light lets you "trample cars and smash through treelines". A 100-ton assault "plows through entire buildings a smaller mech uses as cover", and concrete chunks the size of a small mech "are no obstacle for your big one" — [PC Gamer MW5 Clans review (82/100, Jonathan Bolding)](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/mechwarrior-5-clans-review/)
- Mercenaries: lasers raking woods "leave a trail of falling, burning trees". A dodging mech "shoulder checks a building and obliterates the facade". Mechs "slow to scale inclines or plough through a row of parked cars". Allies also "casually smash whatever you're supposed to defend" — [PC Gamer MW5 Mercenaries review](https://www.pcgamer.com/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-review/)
- Clans: player claims that walking through objects damages your mech while attacking them first avoids it. This is disputed by another player ("you take no damage unless things blow up") — [Steam MW5 Clans discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2000890/discussions/0/4691154661545253974)
- Mercenaries: legged mechs recover from limping after a few seconds. Sarna's reviewer believes this was a co-op compromise and "disagree[s] with it 100%". The "Limp Galore" mod makes the limp permanent — [Sarna: Let's Talk About Mods (2020-09-14)](https://www.sarna.net/news/lets-talk-about-mods-for-mechwarrior-5-mercenaries)
- Knockdown requests and community analysis (Nov 2023, no developer reply):
  - It would be "a pretty massive feature" for a DLC cycle, needing stagger and get-up animations. It is easier in MW5 because the knocked-down units would mostly be AI.
  - Counter-arguments: "stunlock concerns", terrain clipping, and wanting to keep full control.
  - A proposed middle ground is stun or stagger as a weapon damage type to balance ballistics.
  - — [Steam: Mech knockdown](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/3975051032839426182/?l=english)
- Players wanted knockdown for Clans. One argued ballistics currently have "poor damage feedback" because hits don't visibly affect the target's aim. Another linked a MercTech-mod "gyro" that enables knockdown in Mercenaries — [Steam thread, May 2024](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/4355620416677363103)
- A separate thread argues stagger and falls would help ballistic weapons, which some players feel are weak in MW5 — [Steam: I'd really love to see stagger/fall come back (via search)](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/596282228182442514/)
- Clans, Ghost Bear Flash Storm DLC: Elementals (allied battle armour) "latch onto 'Mechs with stripped head armor". A wiped-out Elemental Point redeploys after a one-minute delay per Elemental — [Sarna wiki: MechWarrior 5: Clans](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Clans)
- PC Gamer criticised base Clans for having no infantry and no Elementals on the battlefield — [PC Gamer Clans review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/mechwarrior-5-clans-review/)
- Mercenaries Chaos Reign (May 2026) added Battle Armor as an enemy or unit type — [jeu.video via search](https://jeu.video/en/article/mechwarrior-5-chaos-reign-release); [Sarna wiki](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Mercenaries)

### Inferences
- PGI chose cosmetic physicality: destruction, trampling and building-ploughing, which sell scale and weight. They avoided *gameplay* loss of control: falls, stagger, permanent limps. This is plausibly for co-op fairness and animation cost. The result is that heavy hits have little feedback, and the biggest player complaints follow from that.
- IRONFEUD already has spring secondary motion and footfall impulses, so it can deliver graded stagger (torso spring kick, aim disturbance, a forced recovery step) without the full knockdown-plus-get-up animation cost. Graded stagger also sidesteps the stunlock objection.
- Size-dependent terrain interaction (assaults break cover that lights must go around, lights pass through gaps) is praised as making scale *felt*. It is also a cheap role lever for the weight-class viability goal.

### Gaps
- No primary source on Clans leg-loss behaviour (topple, limp or cripple) or on Clans collision damage.
- I found no developer statement explaining why knockdown was excluded from either game.

## 3. MW5 Clans specifics

### Takeaway
Clans (16 Oct 2024, $49.99, Unreal Engine 5) trades Mercenaries' procedural sandbox for a linear, handcrafted story campaign with mocap cinematics. It has a 5-mech Star with named, skill-differentiated pilots, an OmniPod mechlab gated by per-chassis XP, a research lab instead of scarcity-based salvage, and a much better command layer (F-keys, radial wheel, and an RTS-style "Battle Grid" overhead view).
Handling is called the best in the series, with more felt acceleration and deceleration weight and more distinct weapon ballistics. The AI is still weak but "improved". The late game funnels players into assaults, which critics flag as hurting light and medium viability.

### Cited Findings
**Structure and presentation**
- It is "firmly a story-driven campaign with a sequence of linear, handcrafted missions". There are "no random procedural missions" and "almost never a large open field mission" with objectives in any order. The reviewer spent ~40 h. The Sim Pod lets you replay with unlocks and optional challenges. There are **no mid-mission saves or checkpoints** ("no checkpoint if you die at minute 29 of a 30-minute brawl") — [PC Gamer Clans review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/mechwarrior-5-clans-review/)
- The story uses "motion capture performance focusing on the human characters". It is sometimes uncanny, but it makes you care about "the people in the cockpit". Some set-piece battles had "dozens of units for either side", artillery and aerospace overhead — [PC Gamer Clans review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/mechwarrior-5-clans-review/)
- Press materials promise "over 90 minutes of cinematics", co-op for 1-5 players with crossplay, and UE5. The CEO confirmed Nanite, Lumen, Chaos destruction, World Partition and mocap — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/mechwarrior-5-clans-everything-you-need-to-know); [Gematsu 2024-06 (via search)](https://www.gematsu.com/2024/06/mechwarrior-5-clans-launches-october-3)
- The press-kit feature list: a "Battle Grid, Command Wheel, and streamlined controls" for accessibility, a "revamped MechLab, Hardpoint, and OmniPod system", and a "new pilot progression system" — [mw5clans.com press kit](https://mw5clans.com/resources/press-kit/clans)

**Handling and combat feel**
- "This is the best that handling a BattleMech has ever felt in a game". There is "a real sense of weight to how machines accelerate and decelerate", and each mech has "its own quirks".
- Weapons are distinguished by projectile speed and drop (gauss vs PPC), distinct SRM and LRM flight patterns, and fire-and-forget autocannon vs held-on-target laser vs pulse in between.
- Mount height matters: chest-mounted guns hit a building when the cockpit clears it.
- Footstep and explosion sound is called "anemic".
- — [PC Gamer Clans review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/mechwarrior-5-clans-review/)
- There are two control schemes: Modern (movement follows the reticle, like a shooter) and Classic (legs independent of aim). Chassis upgrades improve top speed, acceleration and torso speed — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/mechwarrior-5-clans-everything-you-need-to-know)
- The upgradable handling stats are acceleration, deceleration, top speed, reverse speed, torso speed, torso angle and turn speed — [Sarna wiki: MW5 Clans](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Clans)
- Clans patch, Nov 2024: "Adjusted weapon recoil when firing multiple weapons at the same time" and Clan Streak SRM6 damage per missile from 2.0 to 2.4 — [mw5clans.com patch notes 2024-11](https://mw5clans.com/news/2024/11/131-mechwarrior-5-clans-update-and-patch-notes)

**Mechlab, progression and research**
- Clan mechs are modular pods (arms, legs, chest, head). Time in a chassis unlocks more preset pod configurations (laser, missile, recon), which are then mixed and matched. The reviewer found it "needlessly confusing" but "a very open and interesting set of choices": 16 chassis versus ~50 in Mercenaries — [PC Gamer Clans review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/mechwarrior-5-clans-review/)
- Sarna's reviewer: each pod section is "limited to a variant configuration that you could unlock" (commenter frustration), "no YAML-levels of customization", and "far greater customization" of OmniMechs than before — [Sarna Clans review 2024-11-03](https://www.sarna.net/news/mechwarrior-5-clans-review-a-new-narrative-in-mechwarrior/)
- Research: scientists generate research points each cycle; each scientist costs 75 Merit, and research consumes salvaged components. The Sim Pod gives 10% Mech and Pilot XP and no research, components or currency, and "'Mechs are not damaged for real" — [Sarna wiki: MW5 Clans](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Clans)
- "The campaign isn't defined by scarcity, but by earning the victories" that get you better toys and more personnel. This contrasts with Mercenaries' "lose it and it's gone" — [PC Gamer Clans review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/mechwarrior-5-clans-review/)
- Player complaints: you can't research everything or max every pilot in one playthrough, and mech XP mostly goes to unlocking OmniPods — [Search summary of Steam/Metacritic user reviews](https://www.metacritic.com/game/mechwarrior-5-clans/)

**Commands and AI**
- "Addressing a big frustration from MechWarrior 5", there are three ways to command: function keys, a middle-mouse radial menu, and the Battle Grid overhead RTS view. - The Command Wheel is "much more full-featured and granular" than in Mercenaries. The Battle Grid is a real-time satellite-style overview for queuing orders. Scan Mode can hack turrets, disarm mines and tag caches, but weapons cannot fire while it is active — [Sarna wiki: MW5 Clans](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Clans)

**Difficulty and weight-class funnel**
- The campaign "funnels you from light 'Mechs to mediums, to heavies, and finally, assaults". The final missions leave "no way to win without bringing as much armor as possible", which turns play into "a gunnery test" and "eliminates part of the power fantasy". The reviewer wants lights viable late but can't say how — [Sarna Clans review](https://www.sarna.net/news/mechwarrior-5-clans-review-a-new-narrative-in-mechwarrior/)
- Wolves of Tukayyid instead opens with three missions on open farmland "where speed is the main defense". Weight limits rise gradually: they exceed 300 t only about halfway through and reach 500 t only in the final two missions — [Sarna Wolves of Tukayyid review](https://www.sarna.net/news/wolves-of-tukayyid-review-raised-by-wolves/)
- Trueborn difficulty (free with Trials of War): "will push your MechWarrior skills to the extreme". A known bug applied increased enemy *shielding stats* to allies too, which reveals that higher difficulty is implemented as damage-reduction stats — [mw5clans.com patch notes](https://mw5clans.com/news/2024/11/131-mechwarrior-5-clans-update-and-patch-notes)

**DLC and post-launch** — [Sarna wiki: MW5 Clans](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Clans); [Wikipedia: MW5 Clans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Clans)
- Trials of War (25 Nov 2024): 5v5 PvP Arena, co-op Horde, 12 hero configurations, Trueborn difficulty, 8 OmniPod sets. It was badly received, so PGI made it free and refunded buyers.
- Ghost Bear Flash Storm (8 May 2025): a 12-mission campaign, 4 OmniMechs and 4 BattleMechs, and a commandable Elemental Point. Reception was positive.
- Wolves of Tukayyid (4 Dec 2025, $19.99): a 20-mission campaign, 6 mechs, Arrow IV, player-called artillery strikes, vectored jump jets, and a drag-and-drop MechLab.
- Players called Arrow IV overpowered, but its large blast also hits your own Star — [Sarna Wolves review](https://www.sarna.net/news/wolves-of-tukayyid-review-raised-by-wolves/)

### Inferences
- Clans shows that **authored missions with escalating tonnage caps** fix pacing but recreate the "bigger is better" endgame unless missions explicitly demand speed or recon. The Wolves DLC's speed-defined opening missions show PGI learned this. This directly supports IRONFEUD's mission-type × lance matrix approach.
- The most praised feel improvements are about **momentum** (acceleration and deceleration weight) and **weapon identity via ballistics**, not about animation. IRONFEUD's procedural gait already targets the first. Projectile speed and drop are cheap levers for the second.
- Battle Grid plus a radial wheel was the single most-cited fix to MW5's AI complaints: giving the player control compensates for weak AI. IRONFEUD's lancemate orders should be drawable (the debug-overlay philosophy) and issuable from an overhead view.
- Per-pilot skills that trigger on situations ("above 70 kph", ECM range, close combat) are a cheap way to make lights and mediums matter. They reward *how* a mech is driven, not its tonnage.

- No confirmed Clans mission count for the base campaign.
- No sales numbers. The publisher only described Flash Storm as performing "nicely" (Wikipedia).

## 4. MW5 Mercenaries specifics: modes, procedural missions, economy, lance, quirks, mods

### Takeaway
Mercenaries is a sandbox loop: travel → take a procedurally generated contract → wreck mechs → repair and refit at hubs → repeat. Faction standing and reputation scale pay and difficulty. It received eight DLCs from 2021 to 2026 plus a ninth announced for Oct 2026, each bundled with free systemic updates (career mode, melee, mech switching, lancemate command reliability, custom difficulty, enemy field refits).
The most persistent complaints are AI quality, enemy spawns and reinforcement attrition, and mission repetition. Mods consistently target exactly those, plus deeper mechlab, quirks and melee.

### Cited Findings
**Loop and economy**
- The "simple, tight, and effective loop" is: travel to a conflict zone, pick a mission and ruin your mechs, take a sidequest, head to industrial worlds to rearm and repair, go back out. Scarcity (LosTech) forces hard choices such as selling a mech that is too expensive to repair. "The missions are chosen from only a few types, but they're always different because each battlefield is procedurally generated and then sprinkled with objectives". Raids ("lightning-fast", destroy specific targets against a huge number of defenders) were the reviewer's favourite. Night and dust storms helped fast flanking lances. Hunting rare mech and weapon icons on the map is "more fun than pursuing the main storyline" — [PC Gamer MW5 Mercenaries review](https://www.pcgamer.com/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-review/)
- The mission types are Assassination, Defense, Demolition, Raid and Warzone, later joined by Arena, Beachhead, Infiltration and Guerrilla Warfare. Variants include Battlefield, Garrison Duty, Objective Raid, Scorched Earth and Targeted Kill.
- Multiple Mission Operations chain contracts, and failing one fails all. High Reward Quests are prescripted chains.
- Conflict Zones have slow, expensive repairs and thin markets. Industrial Hubs have pilots for hire and faster, cheaper repairs.
- Reputation sets base pay and difficulty. Standing is tracked per faction and gives negotiation bonuses and discounts. You can negotiate payout, salvage, damage insurance or airstrikes.
- Kestrel Lancers added time-limited missions, where "lengthy pilot recovery" and refits can bench pilots and mechs for several missions.
- Campaign missions cannot be aborted.
- — [Sarna wiki: MW5 Mercenaries](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Mercenaries)
- Career Mode (Heroes of the Inner Sphere, 27 May 2021): pick a faction start with four free mechs. Quirks are earned through career reputations, e.g. sensor range, speed, cooldown — [Sarna wiki](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Mercenaries)

**DLC timeline with free systemic updates** — [Sarna wiki: MW5 Mercenaries](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Mercenaries)

**AI and spawns**
- The AI is "relatively functional but often comically inept at understanding the terrain". It smashes defend objectives, fires missiles into cliffs, and keeps circling inside its own minimum range with only long-range weapons left. Enemies get stuck, which forces hunts for "one last, tiny tank" — [PC Gamer MW5 Mercenaries review](https://www.pcgamer.com/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-review/)
- Spawns, as players describe them:
  - "two groups of mechs are spawned on the front and back of you";
  - enemies "disappear from my scanner and then reappear behind me";
  - low-difficulty missions turn into "nonstop spawning enemies and Heavy Mech reinforcements";
  - "reinforcements would spawn every couple minutes" on five-star missions.
  - A later review says Heroes of the Inner Sphere made enemies rarely "spawn directly on your ass".
  - — [Search summary of Steam threads](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/7056650139338422640); [Attack of the Fanboy HotIS review (via search)](https://attackofthefanboy.com/reviews/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-heroes-of-the-inner-sphere-review/)

**Mods as a signal of what's missing**
- Sarna's 2020 essential-mods list:
  - "Better Spawns": fewer enemies, arriving by dropship, because missions became attrition — "an essential addition".
  - TTRulez Enemy and Lancemate AI: lancemates stop running through defend targets, and enemies use tactics "beyond charging the player"; "The AI in MechWarrior 5 can be truly atrocious at times".
  - Enemy roster date fix.
  - "Limp Galore" (permanent leg damage limp).
  - Distinct weapon effects ("Weapon feel matters a lot").
  - Weather and dense fog.
  - Music variety.
  - Wished for: jump-jet fix ("do virtually nothing"), design quirks such as "Easy to Repair", running as default.
  - Commenters asked for punching (before 2021) and more mission types.
  - — [Sarna: Let's Talk About Mods (2020-09-14)](https://www.sarna.net/news/lets-talk-about-mods-for-mechwarrior-5-mercenaries)
- YAML (Yet Another Mechlab): MWO-style quirks (armour, structure, cooldown) for hero mechs and later all DLC mechs, a large hardpoint expansion, dual-wield melee, and melee quirks. Players say some YAML combinations add "well over 150% melee damage". It can also disable friendly fire — [YAML Steam changelog](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2549720490); [Steam melee discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/4692280570914398205)
- Coyote's Mission Pack adds procedurally generated mission types: tower defense, multiple objectives, convoy escort, and convoy attack where "the whole convoy fires back". The missions are intentionally harder, up to a brutal difficulty-250 endgame recon. "Better Mission Choices" expands contract generation — [Search summary of Steam threads](https://steamcommunity.com/app/784080/discussions/0/4036976796099634426)
- Steam review themes (Vaporlens, an aggregator built on an automated review summary — treat as indicative only): "Poor AI behavior and pathfinding" is the top negative (78), then dated graphics (48), weak story (42), "Repetitive and uninspired mission design" (39) and "Unrealistic and flawed damage mechanics" (38). Top positives are customisation (98), combat and realism (96) and lore (95). Overall sentiment is about 69% positive — [Vaporlens MW5 Mercenaries](https://vaporlens.app/app/784080/mech_warrior_5_mercenaries)

### Inferences
- Modders consistently add: (1) **smarter, less suicidal enemy AI and lancemates that respect objectives**; (2) **controlled, diegetic reinforcements** (dropship arrivals) instead of attrition spawns behind the player; (3) **more mission types with real objectives** (convoys, multi-objective, tower defense); (4) **per-model quirks and deeper hardpoint and melee freedom**; (5) **persistent damage consequences** (limp). All five map onto IRONFEUD's pillars: perception-limited AI, visible intent, and per-model quirks.
- PGI shipped a big systemic fix with almost every DLC (melee, mech switching, command reliability, custom difficulty, enemy refits, jump vectoring). The base game was a platform that matured over 6+ years. Melee was added late and never integrated with stability or locomotion.

## 5. Reception: critics and players, and Clans vs Mercenaries

### Takeaway
Critics liked both for the core "sim-lite" mech handling. Mercenaries got 80 (PC Gamer) and 8.1 (IGN), but its writing was savaged. Clans scored higher (Metacritic 79, IGN 80, PC Gamer 82): praised for story, cinematics, handling and physics, criticised for losing the sandbox, the OmniPod and research gating, weak AI, no mid-mission saves, and the assault-funnel endgame. Player scores for Clans are lower (Metacritic users 6.5).

### Cited Findings
- On Mercenaries: "a showcase of nostalgic, joyful mech combat" (80/100). It "honors the 30-year MechWarrior legacy very closely". It also has the worst "writing, dialogue, and narrative design" the reviewer could remember. Dropships don't fire, and infantry and aerospace are absent — [PC Gamer MW5 Mercenaries review](https://www.pcgamer.com/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-review/)
- IGN 8.1/10. TechRaptor: "it wasn't just bad, it was disappointing" (thin content at launch) — [Search summary: Epic store / TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/reviews/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-review)
- On Clans (82/100): "a strong story of struggling young warriors alongside the best-ever version of its iconic, lumbering mech combat". "The tradeoff was worth it" versus losing the sandbox — [PC Gamer Clans review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/mechwarrior-5-clans-review/)
- Clans critic score: Metacritic 78-79 (22 reviews). User score 6.5 (60 ratings at the time of the search). OpenCritic top-critic average 76, 63% recommend — [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/mechwarrior-5-clans/); [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/17465/-); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MechWarrior_5:_Clans)
- Sarna: "Certainly the best MechWarrior of them all". Its biggest complaint is no New Game Plus at launch. Jump jets were "useless" before the Wolves of Tukayyid update — [Sarna Clans review](https://www.sarna.net/news/mechwarrior-5-clans-review-a-new-narrative-in-mechwarrior/)
- A player praised how lancemates develop across chapters, making you question the Clan side — [Steam Clans discussion (via search)](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2000890/discussions/0/4629230764944276457)
- Chaos Reign (Mercenaries DLC 8) is "Mostly Positive" on Steam (~180 reviews), praised for cinematics and story — [Vaporlens summary (via search)](https://vaporlens.app/app/4371500/mech_warrior_5_mercenaries_chaos_reign)

### Inferences
- The genre audience forgives a thin story when the mech handling is great, and in 2019 it would not forgive missing melee and bad AI. Clans showed that authored context and cinematics raise critic scores, but players still want sandbox replay. IRONFEUD's planned sandbox-plus-authored-battles architecture fits this split.

## 6. Developer statements on melee, AI and mech feel

### Takeaway
Primary PGI design statements are sparse. The only explicit melee design intent found is in the Call to Arms notes: melee as an "essential part of the skill set", with SRM cooldowns retuned so a strike fits between volleys. Clans messaging centres on accessibility (Battle Grid, wheel, Modern controls) and UE5 destruction and cinematics.

### Cited Findings
- Call to Arms: melee is "the headlining feature". They "monitored its rollout" since Kestrel Lancers and want melee "a more essential part of the skill set". SRM cooldowns were changed so there is "room for a full melee strike between SRM cooldown cycles". "We want to allow 'LosTech' weapons to fall on the stronger side of the balance curve" — [mw5mercs.com 2022-06](https://mw5mercs.com/news/2022/06/73-call-to-arms-launch-date-intel)
- The Kestrel Lancers melee patch framed melee as a tool for "when attacking would otherwise overheat you" — [Gematsu](https://gematsu.com/2021/09/mechwarrior-5-mercenaries-expansion-legend-of-the-kestrel-lancers-and-free-melee-update-launch-september-23)
- Clans: the CEO said UE5 is used "in every way we possibly can" (Nanite, Lumen, Chaos, mocap, World Partition) — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/mechwarrior-5-clans-everything-you-need-to-know)
- Clans Trueborn difficulty: "success depends on careful preparation and execution" — [mw5clans.com patch notes](https://mw5clans.com/news/2024/11/131-mechwarrior-5-clans-update-and-patch-notes)

### Inferences
- PGI's framing makes melee a **heat-management and brawler-rhythm tool**, not a separate combat layer. In a game with heat shutdowns (IRONFEUD has them), heat-free melee is a natural pressure valve for overheated brawlers. It is also a reason for the AI to close distance when it is hot.
